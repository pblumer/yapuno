# Yapuno product vision

**Yapuno — Your crew. Your chat.**

A warm, playful and **closed** space to communicate for children and teens. No public stranger discovery, engagement feed, ads, phone number or child-managed passwords. Parent/guardian-approved contacts and devices are the default; children have age-appropriate autonomy and dignity.

**Primary users:** children roughly 5–16 years old using iPhones and Android phones (or Wi-Fi-only devices). Secondary users: guardians managing approvals and recovery. Potential future opt-in communities: private school-class groups and clubs, then verified school organizations, as a separate trust boundary from each family's tenant. See [Communities & Classrooms](../planning/communities-classrooms.md); no direct-contact permission is implied by community membership.

**First Hello:** two children, each with a parent-approved device, can exchange text messages through mutually approved contacts via Wi-Fi. Other tenants, unauthorized devices and contacts must not see, send or subscribe to these messages.

**Mobile First Experience:** start from the smallest touchscreen; use a simple, friendly UI, readable copy, large touch controls, graceful keyboard/reconnect behavior and optional Home Screen installation. The full MFE contract is [here](mobile-first-experience.md).

**Do not confuse aspirations with claims.** An internet connection is required, school Wi-Fi may block services, child-safe moderation is never perfect, and Yapuno cannot stop someone using other apps or capturing a screen.

**Safety and privacy:** protection is based on closed contact boundaries, reporting, measured guidance and transparent, age-appropriate rules. Guardians administer access without automatically reading every private message. Retention must be bounded and incident evidence carefully restricted. Pilot requires independent security, privacy and child-usability review.

**Out of scope for the first release:** public discovery, full video, social feed, ad SDKs, third-party trackers, AI cloud content monitoring, unrestricted family chat access.
